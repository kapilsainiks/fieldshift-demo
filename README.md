# Field Shift on Fieldproof

Team Malapert's version of Fieldproof for the NASA Space Apps 2026 "Field Shift" challenge.

This is a copy of Alex Picon's Fieldproof app (https://github.com/alexpicon/malapert-nasa-challenge, live at https://alexpicon.github.io/malapert-nasa-challenge/), updated to his gh-pages commit b18726e with the NASA map views (crop or rest from HLS, heat from ECOSTRESS) and the seasons what-if,, told the way our pitch tells it. It drops the 12-acre strip trial and the "try it small" framing. Every plan ends on what would change the answer, and the proof page stays honest about what the past can and cannot show. The engine, data and everything else are Alex's work, unchanged.

Changed files: `index.html`, `js/home.js`, `js/plan.js`, `js/plan-util.js`, `js/proof.js`, `js/proof-util.js`, `css/plan.css`.
